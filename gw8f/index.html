Learning Goals:

  • Develop an intuition for how eigenvalues and eigenvectors relate to the motion of multiple springs-masses in vibrations
  • Generate the free response in vibrations as a sum of the individual vibration modes from the eigenvalues and eigenvectors
  • Change the eigenvalues and eigenvectors and the free response of a system by modifying physical parameters (e.g. m, k)
