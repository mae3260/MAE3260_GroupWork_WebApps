Quick rules
  • Use log frequency ω [rad/s]. Magnitude = 20 log₁₀|G(jω)| dB; phase = angle of G(jω).
  • One decade is a 10× frequency change, such as 1 to 10 rad/s.
  • Multiply factors: add their dB magnitudes and phase angles.
  • First-order zero/pole: +20/-20 dB/decade and +90°/-90°.
  • Real factors change near ωc; complex pairs change by ±40 dB/decade and ±180°. ωn sets location; ζ shapes the curve.
