Self-Driving Car: Lane and Speed Control

A car drives down a straight 50 m road, takes a 90° left corner with a 10 m radius, then drives another 60 m straight. A steering controller and a speed controller keep the car in a 3.7 m lane. This page is the browser version of the MATLAB live script MAE3260_SDC_lanespeedcontrol_2025a: pick a controller type and its gains with the controls, and the simulation, the printed results and the three figures update the way they do in the live script.

The model (discrete time, Euler steps of dt = 0.005 s, as in the script):

The car position is measured with noise (standard deviation 0.001 m on each of x and y). The wheel reference is found by looking H = 0.1 s ahead on the center of the lane: the heading change needed to point at that point gives ω, and δref = atan(ωL/V). The steering controller turns the wheel-angle error into a torque on the steering column (J = 1), limited to ±500 N·m, with a 3 N·m friction torque opposing the motion. The wheel can turn at most ±30°. The speed controller turns the speed error into a thrust force F on the car (m = 1400 kg, drag coefficient b = 100).

Steering controller types (e = reference − output; with “internal feedback” the derivative term, and for the PID version also the proportional term, acts on the output θ instead of the error):

Type Torque T
PID Ki·∫e + Kp·e + Kd·de/dt
PID (internal fdbk) Ki·∫e − Kp·θ − Kd·dθ/dt
PD Kp·e + Kd·de/dt
PD (internal fdbk) Kp·e − Kd·dθ/dt

Speed controller types: P: F = KpV·e; PI: F = KiV·∫e + KpV·e; PI (internal fdbk): F = KiV·∫e − KpV·v.

Lane and Speed Control

Choose the controllers and gains, then press RUN SIMULATION: the car drives the course in real time (1× speed) and leaves its path behind it, and the results print when it finishes. STOP ANIMATION freezes the car where it is. The gains must stay inside the slider ranges (the script prints a warning otherwise).
