Passive Suspension Design

Learning Goals:

  • Understand how different input-output pairs of the same system create different transfer functions
  • Choose a model parameter to simultaneously meet multiple performance objectives
  • Relate frequency domain and time domain representations of multiple system responses
  • Design and verify a system using passive techniques (i.e. selecting system parameters)

We model a car's suspension as a quarter-car system: a mass m (the car body) connected to the road through a spring k and damper b in parallel. The road surface height over time is r(t). Three variables describe the response: the car's position y(t), its acceleration a(t) = ÿ(t), and the shock travel d(t) = y(t) − r(t).

GIVEN · m = 325 kg · k = 500 N/m · R₀ = 5 cm (both bumpy road and speed bump)

Two scenarios guide the design:

Bumpy road — r(t) = R₀ sin(ωt):
